Optional fonts
==============
The app looks best with these two free fonts (SIL Open Font License), but works without them
(it falls back to Segoe UI, which every Windows computer has).

To add them, download from fonts.google.com and put these files in this folder:
  AtkinsonHyperlegible-Regular.ttf
  AtkinsonHyperlegible-Bold.ttf

Atkinson Hyperlegible was designed by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision.
